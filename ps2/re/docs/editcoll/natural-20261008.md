# editcoll: natural typed access (2026-10-08)

## Replaced

- `CEditCollision::Copy`: the counting loop tests `poly[i].area_kind`
  instead of stepping a byte offset over `this->poly`.
- `CEditCollision::AreaXZ` and `OverlapXZ`: the polygons are walked with a
  `CCPoly *` (`poly->vertex[n][c]`, `poly->vertex` passed to
  `mgApplyMatrixN`, `poly++`) instead of a `float (*)[4]` stepped by five rows.

## Kept

- `CEditCollision::Copy`, copy loop: `src` and `dst` come from byte offsets
  over `poly` and `dest.poly`. `&poly[copied]` and `&dest.poly[dst_no]`
  exchange the source-offset and destination-offset registers.
- `CEditCollision::PickupVerticalPoly`: the plane search reads
  `this->poly` at a byte offset. `this->poly[j]`, in either a `for` or the
  original guarded `do`/`while`, exchanges the offset and `cur` registers.
- `CollisionRow` copies of whole `CCPoly` records are quadword row copies.
