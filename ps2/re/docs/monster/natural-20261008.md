# monster: natural typed access (2026-10-08)

## Replaced

- `GetMonsterTable` and `CMonsterMan::GetReferPtr2` test the end of
  `base_monster_define` with `entry->name[0]` instead of
  `((s8 *) entry->name)[0]`; `name` is already `char`.
- `CMonsterMan::LoadReferMonsterFile`: the sword effects' placement `new`
  takes the `u_long128 *` from `Alloc` without a same-type cast.
- `active[i]` and `other` (`CActiveMonster *`) call the inherited `GetEntryObjectPos`
  without the `(CCharacter2 *)` upcast.
- Shared monster model: `main_frame_info` takes `motion[0].frame_info` without the `(s32)`
  conversion now that the field is `tagFRAME_INF *`.
- Hit reactions test `prim->param->hit_flags` without `(s16)` now that the field is `s16`.

## Kept
- Hit texture rectangle: `HitRectangle copy = *(HitRectangle *) &rect` loads the four
  coordinates as one aligned record before the field stores.
- Photo/object effect positions: `((EffectVector *) row)->qw = ((EffectVector *) position)->qw`
  copies a `float[4]` as one quadword (arrays cannot be assigned).
