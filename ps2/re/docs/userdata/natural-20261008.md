# userdata natural access (2026-10-08 night)

Byte-identical object (`check_objects userdata`: 0xBC1C bytes, 1081 relocations).

## Replaced
- `CHARA_DATA::defence` is `u16`: every reader (`GetDefenceVol`, `RefreshParamater`, menucls1,
  menusys) loads it with `lhu`; the stores are unchanged. `CopyDataFish` reads the `u16`
  `CDataBreedFish` abilities (gamedata) directly.
- `UseNpcAbility`: `(u8 *) GetPartyNPCData()` plus `[ability_no + 0x32]` becomes
  `NPC_BASE_DATA::ability_cost[ability_no]`.
- `SearchEquip`: three `offset += sizeof(CGameDataUsed)` byte walks become indexed loops over
  `active_item`, `equip` and `robo_data.parts`. They match only when all three loops reuse one
  counter `i` (distinct `j`/`k` counters swap counter and offset registers in loops two and three).
- `GetNumSameItem`: five byte walks become indexed loops. Retail's registers come out only with
  the bag, character and part loops sharing `i`, both inner loops sharing `j`, `used_data[i]`
  indexed directly (an `item` local swaps it with the offset induction value) and a `data` local
  for `chara_data[i]` (indexing `chara_data[i]` directly shrinks the function by two words).
- `DeleteErekiFish`: `(u8 *) fish + off` becomes `fish[i]` in a `for` loop.
- `LeaveMonicaItemCheck`: `(u8 *) monica + off + 0x2C` becomes `monica->active_item[i]` in a `for`
  loop; naming the element in an `active` local swaps it with the offset induction register.
- `GetAquariumFish0`: `(u8 *) aquarium + slot * sizeof + 4/6` becomes `aquarium->fish_tank[slot]`.
- `CBattleCharaInfo::Initialize` clears `sizeof(CBattleCharaInfo)`.

- `CFishingTournament::EntryFish`: the free place is filled through
  `entry[i].item_no`, `.size` and `.weight` instead of `short` words at
  `(i << 3) + (int) this`. The parameters are named `item_no` and `size`
  after the values the only caller (menusys) passes.
- `CBattleCharaInfo::GetSpecialStatus` and `GetPalletNo` return
  `weapon_param[slot].special` and `.pallet_no` instead of reading the
  object at `slot * 0x1C` plus 0x48 and 0x4C.
- `CGameDataUsed::TransToData`: the packed fish bytes copy `data[i]` without a
  `(signed char *)` view of the `char *` parameter.
- `CBattleCharaInfo::UseActiveItem`: `int target[2]` is a `CItemUseTarget` declared after
  `item_no` is read; the constructor gives the `-1` store into `type`.
- `fish_tank`, `sub_tank` and `breed_tank` are `CGameDataUsed` arrays; `Init` is called on
  `tank[i]` without the same-type `(CGameDataUsed *) &` cast.
- `CGameDataUsed::GetName`: `MenuBigNum` entries are already `char *`; `SetChrNo`: the outer
  `(CGameDataUsed *)` on `equip`/`&parts[0]` was same-type.

## Kept
- `CBattleCharaInfo::GetNowAccessAbs` (human) and `GetNowAccessWHp` (ridepod):
  `(COMMON_GAGE *) ((u8 *) p + 0x10) + 1`. Retail adds 0x10 and then 8 in two `addiu`s;
  `&equip[slot].data.weapon.abs`, a `WEAPON_USED *` local and `&equip->data.robopart.whp` all fold
  into one 0x18 offset (function four bytes shorter).
- Casts of `chara_data` (a `void *` that is a `CHARA_DATA`, `ROBO_DATA` or `MOS_CHANGE_PARAM` by
  `chara_type`) are ordinary downcasts, not puns.
