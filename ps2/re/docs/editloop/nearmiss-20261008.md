# EditDraw near-miss notes

`EditDraw` is native and matches; the source forms it depends on are in
[notes.md](notes.md). Facts established while closing its last eight words:

- The retail `MainScene` pointer at `0x37D328` is this unit's file-local
  `CScene *MainScene`, not a scene object.
- `MainScene`, `LoopMode`, `EditDrawFlag`, `EditDrawCancelFlag` and
  `WalkChara` are retail-local static definitions; their visibility does
  not affect the function's code.
- The texture-group counts and indices are signed `int`: unsigned reverse
  counts, a shared count/map index or an unsigned group index change the
  loop code, and `index <= count - 1` loop limits exceed the 0xD70 extent.
- The eight-word residual of the direct `mds_list_set.GetTextureBlockNo`
  call form (count copied to its saved register before the test, and the
  forward loop's count/byte-offset registers exchanged) is removed by the
  inline `CScene::GetTextureBlockNo` accessor and a named element index.
