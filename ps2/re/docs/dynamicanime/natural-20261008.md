# dynamicanime: natural typed access (2026-10-08)

## Replaced

- `CDynamicAnime::ResetPosition`, `CDynamicAnime::PreCollision` and
  `CDAColPipe::CheckHit`: `now_vertex`, `init_vertex`
  (`sceVu0FVECTOR *`) and `lw_matrix`, `inverse_matrix` (`sceVu0FMATRIX`)
  are passed without `(float (*)[4])` casts; their types already decay to
  the parameter type.

## Kept

- `(sceVu0FVECTOR *) memory->Alloc(...)` converts fresh stack allocations
  to the vertex arrays they hold.
