# inventmn: index widths in ResetAddress and MenuInventKey

`CInventUserData::ResetAddress` needs a signed `int` photo index: an unsigned
word index changes the compare opcodes, and short indices enlarge the
function. The established layouts apply: thirty `USER_PICTURE_INFO` records
of 0x18 bytes with the image pointer at +0x14, and thirty 0x2000-byte pixel
rows at `CInventUserData + 0xD60`.

`MenuInventKey` needs a signed `int` list-row index and card counter: unsigned
or short/signed-byte widths enlarge the function. Its card-list tops are two
signed words. The matching form of its shared loop counter is in
[notes.md](notes.md) under "Matching-dependent source forms".
