Base `d4e37212`, image `chronicletwo_dev:sf-63f7a9e`, canonical flags and

## Unit optimization level

Retail `TexAnime` performs global common-subexpression elimination without
strength reduction: `group * 4` is computed once per main-loop iteration and
spilled (`sw/lw 0xF0(sp)`) for the frame and list accesses at the loop end,
`&frame[group]` is materialized before the comparison that stores through it,
and the first loop still shifts its index every iteration. Without the global
optimizer no value survives those joins; with the full `-O3` optimizer the
first loop is strength-reduced (`addiu s1,s1,4`) and `Initialize`'s loop is
rotated. `#pragma optimization_level 2` produces exactly the retail mixture.

Applied once to the whole code section, level 2 reproduces every matched
function in the unit; the five `global_optimizer off` regions it replaces are
no longer needed. `opt_strength_reduction off` alone breaks `Initialize`
(18/28) and `NewTexAnimeGroupData` (20/72); strength reduction plus loop
invariants off matches the unit but leaves `TexAnime` at 64 words; level 2
with `opt_propagation off` breaks `texSCROLL`. The final
`optimization_level 1` before the data remains for `__sinit_mg_tanime.cpp`.

### Deferred code generation

`TexAnime` and `NewTexAnimeData` use class templates (`mgRect<int>`,
`CList<mgCTexAnimeData>`), and MWCC defers their code generation until the
next top-level declaration begins. Pragmas placed between such a function's
closing brace and the next declaration therefore govern it. Under the former
layout `TexAnime` was compiled by the `global_optimizer off` that precedes
`mgCTextureAnime::Initialize`, whatever surrounded the draft itself; its own
pragma pair had no effect (an explicit `global_optimizer on` before the body
still gave the 840-word result). A trailing declaration proved this in a
private probe only. Small template-free functions (`Initialize`) take the state
in force at their own definition. Minimal reproductions are in
`.private/nmmisc/src/mini5.cpp` … `mini12.cpp`.

Rectangles: retail constructs eight `mgRect<int>` objects with distinct frame
slots `0x2C0`–`0x330`. Two are built after the indexed path's end values,
two after the true-colour path's end values, and two at the start of the
VRAM draw, of which only the first is drawn with. `mgRect<int>`'s default
constructor is empty (other units declare uninitialized rectangles), so
these are explicit zero constructions. The palette and copy rectangles are
argument temporaries, which is why they follow the named ones (`0x320`,
`0x330`), and the two palette TEX0 values come last (`0x340`/`0x348`).

Period magnitudes: `if (p < 0.0f) p = -p;`, its `>= 0` empty-then form and
mixed forms keep one nop in the wrong place (an extra nop at the indexed X
join, one missing at each Y join, one missing at the wave entry). The
conditional-expression form gives exactly retail's nops at all four joins.

`current`: retail keeps `&now[group]` in the last spill slot (`0x130`), after
the indexed path's load-CSE temporaries, so it is the optimizer's address CSE
of `now[group]`, not a user pointer; writing `now[group]` directly moves it
there.

Rejected (no change from 48 unless noted): `node` shared with the first loop
(1204: the first loop then spills it), `node` or `data` declared in the loop
body, `node` declared after `data`, `opt_lifetimes off`,
`opt_dead_assignments off`.

Spill slots: retail orders them `group 0xA0, src_left 0xB0, src_top 0xC0,
dest_left 0xD0, dest_top 0xE0, group*4 0xF0, node 0x100, src end CSEs
0x110/0x120, &now[group] 0x130`. Assigning `node = now[group]` before the null
test placed `node` at `0xB0`; testing `now[group]` first and then loading
`node` gives retail's slot order exactly.

A 96-variant sweep of the head (combined, split or reordered null tests) and
tail (`wait` declared in place, at loop scope or absent; `else if` or nested
`if`; counter cleared before or after advancing; `++` or `+= 1`) finds only
the head form above significant; clearing the counter after advancing costs
77–105 words. Sixteen comparison spellings for the two expiry tests give 18
or 19 words.

### Expiry branches (the last 18 words)

An LLDB capture of the allocator (`.private/nmmisc/ra/`, the framework under
`.private/nmsim/ra/`) explains the tail. Before spilling, the first colouring
already gives retail's registers; after spill code is inserted, the reloaded
`node`'s `->next` value is a fresh backend temporary (v850) numbered above the
`this + group*4` base (v847), so it is coloured first, takes `v1` and pushes
the base to `a0`, `wait` to `a1` and `&frame[group]` (IRO CSE v37) to `v1`.
A declared `next` local is an optimizer-level variable with a low number, so it
is coloured after the base and `wait` and lands in `a0`, as in retail; the
whole tail then matches. Assigning the advanced record back to `node` instead
stores into its spill slot (0x1468 body) and is rejected.

`NewTexAnimeData` (placement-new park, not worked here) drops from 6/32 to
1/32 words under level 2: only `beqz v0` (retail) versus `beqz s0` remains.

## Inherited accessor workarounds under level 2

Before df21a954, the matched `GetEmptyGroup`, `SearchGroupName` and
`NewTexAnimeGroupData` carried workarounds from the former
`global_optimizer off` layout: a
volatile read of `group_num` and byte offsets from `this` (`0x64` for `list`,
`0x124` for `name`). Under level 2, `group_num`, `list[i]` and `name[i]`
reproduce both search functions exactly, and `node->pGetData() == NULL`
reproduces `NewTexAnimeGroupData`'s record test. With all three applied, the
draft comparison still matches all 32 unguarded functions. The natural
group-count/list/name accesses and `pGetData()` test were
applied in df21a954 after complete-unit validation. The following list-slot
address remains the unresolved exception.

`NewTexAnimeGroupData`'s list-slot pointer has no natural replacement yet.
Retail computes `group * 4 + this` before adding `0x64`. `&list[group]`,
`list + group`, `group + list`, `&list[0] + group`, `&this->list[group]` and a
reference to `list[group]` all add `this` first (1/72 words). Indexing
`list[group]` directly gets the order right but swaps the slot and head
registers (11/72), and stepping a pointer from `list` costs 32/72. Loading the
head as `list[group]` beside a `&list[group]` slot stays at 1/72, and taking the
slot after that load gives 2/72.

and `linked_data`, keeping them distinct from the outer draw-loop values.

level-2 pragma and the `NewTexAnimeData` guard are retained.

pass. The complete mg_tanime object hash equals the lane baseline.
