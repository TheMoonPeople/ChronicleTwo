# memcard: natural typed access (2026-10-08)

## Replaced

- `CMemoryCardManager::InitSaveFileInfoTable`: clears `dir_table[entry_no]`
  in a `for` loop instead of reaching each entry through a
  `CMemoryCardManager *` displaced by a byte offset.
- `CMemoryCardManager::Initialize`: the save buffer's placement `new` takes
  the `u_long128 *` from `Alloc` without a same-type cast.
- `sceMcOpen` and `sceMcMkdir` take `const char *name` (sce/libmc.h), as in the SDK; the
  card paths in `MakeDir`, `SaveToMc`, `LoadFromMc`, `SaveAlbum` and `SaveOamkeFile` are
  `char[0x80]`, the `at_1315__3`/`at_1954` name constants are `char`, and the 16 `(char *)` /
  `(const unsigned char *)` casts on them are dropped.
- `CMemoryCardManager::GetSaveFileInfoFromMc`: `strcmp(save_buffer->version, "dc2Ver4")` compares the
  `SAVEDATA_FORMAT` version string instead of `(char *) save_buffer`.

## Kept

- `(char *) icon_sys.title_name` in `CopyMCBrowserName` and `sprintf`: `sceMcIconSys::title_name`
  is the SDK's `unsigned char` array (Shift-JIS bytes).
- `MakeCheckDigit(0, (char *) &save_buffer->save_data, ...)` and the
  `write_buffer`/`read_buffer` assignments treat save records as raw bytes
  for checksums and card I/O.
