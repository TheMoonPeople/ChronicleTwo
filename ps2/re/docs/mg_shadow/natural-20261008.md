# mg_shadow: natural typed access (2026-10-08)

## Replaced

- `mgCShadowMDT::CreateFace`: the triangle indices are walked as
  `int *vertex` over `source->index`, reading the position index of each of
  the three vertices (`vertex[0]`, `vertex[3]`, `vertex[6]`) and stepping
  `vertex += 9`, instead of `*(int *) (vertex + 0xC)` byte offsets.
- The face section is read through `MDT_FACES`: the record count is
  `faces->prim_num` and the first `FACES_ID` follows the header at
  `faces + 1`, instead of `*(int *) (table + 8)` and `table + 0x10`.
- `mgCDrawManager::packet_memory`/`data_memory` are already `mgCMemory *`; the same-type casts
  on the locals are dropped.

## Kept

- `mgCShadowMDT::CreatePacket`: DMA tags are built as `u_int` words, the
  face data is addressed through its uncached mirror as an integer
  (`face_cursor | 0x20000000`), and packet sizes are byte differences
  between the `u_int` cursor and the `u_long128` packet start.
- The VU header packet mixes integer and float words (`head[20]` as an
  integer, `((float *) head)[21..23]` and `[56..]` as floats) and copies
  quadwords and matrices into it through `u_long128` and `sceVu0FVECTOR`
  views.
- `sceVu0CopyMatrix((sceVu0FVECTOR *) &vu[6], ...)`: `vu` is the `u_long128` VU memory image;
  SDK quadword argument.
- `mgRENDER_INFO::render_params` is `u_int[4]` but holds three floats and a word: `CreateRenderInfoPacket`
  reads `[0..2]` through `(float *)` and `[3]` as an integer. Splitting it into `float[3]` plus a
  `u_int` needs the same edit in mg_sprite and water (excluded tonight), which index `[3]`.
