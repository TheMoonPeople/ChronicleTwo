## Depth-buffer assignment results

The double-buffer depth-base fields are unsigned nine-bit members of the
existing eight-byte `sceGsZbuf` register type. Retail writes the second
context's ZBP, masks that assignment result again, and uses it for the first
context. The natural expression is:

```cpp
mgDBuff.draw0.zbuf1.bits.zbp =
    mgDBuff.draw1.zbuf1.bits.zbp = frame_buf1 * 2;
```

The inner assignment's narrowed result explains retail's extra `andi` at
`mgInit+0x3C4`. Independent assignments of `frame_buf1 * 2` omitted that
instruction. The chain improves 168/652 to 32/652 without changing the
0xA30-byte extent. The previous apparent conversion-loop entry difference
was principally the resulting four-byte displacement: the chain restores
both dither loops and their branch destinations. No dither-loop edit is
needed in this round.

Retail then copies the complete draw0 ZBUF to `mgZBUF_2` before `mgZBUF_1`.
`mgZBUF_1 = mgZBUF_2 = mgDBuff.draw0.zbuf1` produces the correct two stores
and temporary registers, reducing the score to 27/652. These use the
compiler-generated assignment; no special member function is written.

The render-info context copies require sequential direct construction of
the two actual register snapshots:

```cpp
sceGsZbuf first_zbuf(mgZBUF_1);
mgRenderInfo.draw_env[0].zbuf = first_zbuf;
sceGsZbuf second_zbuf(mgZBUF_2);
mgRenderInfo.draw_env[1].zbuf = second_zbuf;
```

This restores the stack +0x50/+0x58 snapshots and retail's `a1`/`v1` use.
Both snapshots still carry the complete eight-byte register. The score
becomes 19/652, with the whole context window exact. Initializing the clear
flag before the background channels reduces one further word, to 18/652.
The remaining differing instructions lie only at +0x320..+0x36C; the region
from +0x370 to the end agrees in the masked comparison.

## Initialization hygiene and negative evidence

The 18 words still concern scheduling of `frame_buf1`, the four background
channels, the clear flag, and initial FRAME/ZBP operands. Alternative flag
positions and independent background-store orders reach 18 or 19 words;
none reaches zero. Native definitions of the background vector, framebuffer
indices or double buffer do not improve the 19-word trial. FRAME's named
field view also leaves 19 words. Reversing frame initialization gives 30;
resetting background before the frame-size calculation gives 36; using the
existing background API gives 441/652.

Unnamed ZBUF construction and const references give 26/652. A snapshot
array gives 368/664 and extra storage. Const-value casts give 360/652.
The retained sequential direct construction supplies the exact context code.

The inherited initialization still contains scalar/quadword register casts
and therefore cannot be activated under the promotion rules. Lawful
aggregate TEST/ALPHA initialization gives 407/654; all four register
aggregates give 72/652 with new literal loads and stack slots. Scoping those
aggregates leaves 72/652. GIF aggregate initialization gives 589/666, and
C++ value initialization gives 594/664. A memcpy TEST initializer gives
391/656; member pointers or references for TEST/ALPHA give 374/652. These
forms are not retained.

Private floating selectors for zero, 128.0f, and both together leave 19/652.
There is no accepted compiler-profile row. Helper-mask trials already in
the earlier notes were not repeated.

An exploratory SDK change adds packed
`value` views for GIF, TEX1 and TEXA while preserving the original field
widths and names. The private header compiles, its register-size assertions
pass, and all 97 existing native mglib functions stay exact in the diagnostic.
Replacing all five mgInit register casts with value-member assignments gives
553/652 and a 0xA10-byte body, so the patch is not a matching solution.
Alignment probes increase `sizeof({char, sceGifTag})` from 0x18 to 0x20,
confirming the GIF view changes type alignment; other units have not been
validated against it. The proposal is exploratory and is not
applied to the shared SDK header or committed.

## Framebuffer packet evidence

The packet at `mgSetPkFrameBuffer+0x270` has four DMA/VIF words at +0x00,
four GIF-tag words at +0x10, and six 16-byte A+D entries at +0x20..+0x7F.
The entries are TEXFLUSH, FRAME_1, XYOFFSET_1, SCISSOR_1, SCANMSK and
TEXFLUSH. The total extent is 0x80 bytes, or 32 words.

Two private typed representations were tested: a register-value union in
an array of A+D entries, and six named typed register entries. Their FRAME,
XYOFFSET and SCISSOR assignments use implicit copies, removing the
XYOFFSET/SCISSOR scalar casts. Both give 262/424, worse than the retained
252/424. Adding aggregate TEX1 initialization gives 285/424. No packet-type
change is retained.

Retail reserves the packet using endpoint subtraction followed by signed
byte-count division by four. The available SDK packet header and retail
packet exports provide no count/current getter that could supply this
expression. `sizeof` folds to 32 and cannot reproduce the signed correction
window. Address subtraction is excluded by the source rules, and
no extra helper, raw cursor induction or replacement assembly is introduced.
The reservation expression and later metadata allocation remain parked.
